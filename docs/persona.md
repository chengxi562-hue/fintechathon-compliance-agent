## Choice

**Primary persona = young white-collar with some investable assets**  
（有一定可投资产的年轻白领，不是在校大学生）

Rationale (research-lite, for prize narrative):
1. **Regulatory fit**: suitability / 适当性 rules bite harder when risk products and larger tickets appear — better showcase for the four-gate “合规官”.
2. **Bank KPI**: AUM depth and wealth conversion matter more for young professionals than for campus stipend users.
3. **Demo contrast**: same utterance (“帮我买个高收益理财”) can be **blocked** (risk mismatch / aggressive script) or **allowed** (matched R2 product + compliant wording) — clearer auto-eval story.
4. **Cross-scene**: salary inflow, rent/subscription, and wealth transfer are natural for white-collar; student spend is thinner for 跨场景联动.

Students remain a **secondary** persona for future A/B copy, not the W0 script lead.
